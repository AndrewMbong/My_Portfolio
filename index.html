04 / Ethics in IT

Protect the people behind the data.

My approach to an information security incident is to limit harm, preserve evidence, communicate responsibly, and follow established procedures.

The dilemma

A compromised employee account is used to access a database containing customer information. The organization must respond to the security risk while balancing operational and reputational pressures with its responsibility to affected people.

My guiding priorities: privacy, honesty, accountability, responsibility, and harm prevention.

My response framework

  • Secure or disable the compromised account and check for ongoing unauthorized activity.
  • Preserve relevant logs and evidence.
  • Determine which systems and information may have been accessed.
  • Report through appropriate internal channels and follow incident-response procedures.
  • Consider required notifications under applicable laws, contracts, and policies.
  • Identify root causes and improve authentication, access controls, training, and monitoring.
